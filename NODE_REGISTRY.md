| **MergeMat** | `fr.inria.built-in.MergeMat` | Registered | "Merge Material" — combine two materials, like classic Nuke's MergeMat (added 2026-06-24, `79d1fb988`). Output plugs into a geo's **mat** input; its main use is layering multiple **Project3D** projections onto one piece of geometry (e.g. a front projection `over` a side projection). The per-fragment compositing of the layered projections is done by **ScanlineRender** (flattens the MergeMat tree into ≤4 projection layers — B first, then A over it — composited in the GLSL shader; a lone Project3D = 1 layer so single-projection renders are unchanged) and mirrored in the **3D viewport** (fixed-function **multi-pass**: one alpha-blended pass per layer with GL_LEQUAL + alpha-test so cropped pixels let lower layers show through; converted for sphere/cube/cylinder/ReadGeo-mesh/Card3D). The MaterialProvider params delegate to A-then-B so single-material consumers (Cycles) still get a sensible material. LIMITS: cap 4 layers (texture-unit budget); blend ops — over exact, replace/plus approximated, stencil/mask/min/max → over in the viewport (the render does them properly); Cycles falls back to the foreground material (no layered projection yet — a future MixShader combine). |
